# Sales Analysis Insights

## Category Analysis
- Technology generated the highest total sales among the three categories.
- Furniture showed steady sales across the analyzed period.
- Office Supplies had comparatively lower sales values.

## Regional Analysis
- Sales performance varies across the four regions.
- The West and South regions contributed strongly to overall sales.
- Regional comparison helps identify differences in sales performance.

## Monthly Analysis
- Sales changed from month to month.
- Higher-value Technology and Furniture orders contributed to stronger months.
- Monthly PivotTable makes it easier to identify sales patterns.

## Conclusion

PivotTables provide a quick and effective way to summarize sales data by category, region, and month. The analysis can help identify sales patterns and support basic business decision-making.
